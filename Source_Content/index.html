About the Fest

About Technofest

Technofest 2026 is the annual technical and cultural celebration of Anand Institute of Higher Technology (AIHT), an autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. From cutting-edge technical competitions to fun-filled non-technical activities, the fest gives students an opportunity to showcase their skills, discover new talents and compete with peers.

Whether you're a coder, an engineer, a creative thinker or just someone ready to take on a challenge, Technofest 2026 at AIHT's OMR campus has something for everyone. Come prepared to innovate, compete and create.

Participation Guidelines

How Participation Works

Choose your category and select the events that suit you. The registration form will guide you through the rest.

⚡

Technical Event

Students choosing the Technical Event category will select one or more technical events in the registration form. Showcase your engineering and analytical skills.

🎭

Non-Technical Event

Students choosing the Non-Technical Event category will select one or more non-technical events. Creativity, fun and teamwork are at the heart of these challenges.

🌐

Both

Students choosing Both can select one or more technical events AND one or more non-technical events. The ultimate Technofest experience.

💬

Important: Choose your participation category carefully before selecting your events. The registration form will show the relevant event options based on your selection.

Get Registered

How to Register

Registration is fast and simple through the official Technofest 2026 Google Form.

🔍 STEP 01

Choose Your Events

Explore the technical and non-technical events. Decide which competitions you want to participate in — Technical, Non-Technical or Both.

📝 STEP 02

Fill the Registration Form

Click Register Now and complete the official Technofest 2026 Google Form. Enter your details, select your category and choose your events.

🎯 STEP 03

Get Ready

Check the event information on this website and prepare for 30 September 2026. Your journey to Technofest begins now.
